Q - 5 : Use ChatGPT to suggest 3 new features that could be engineered from a dataset of online food 
        orders (like Zomato orders: order time, restaurant, cuisine, delivery time, rating). List the
        features and explain how each could help a data science model.<br><br><em><strong>Hint:
        </strong> Paste the column names into ChatGPT and ask for feature engineering ideas.</em>


Answer : 


Suppose the dataset contains:

order_time
restaurant
cuisine
delivery_time
rating
1. Order Hour

Feature: Extract the hour from order_time.

Example:

14:30 → 14
20:45 → 20

How it helps:
The model can learn whether delivery times, ratings, or order patterns change during lunch and dinner hours.

2. Delivery Speed Category

Feature: Convert delivery_time into categories such as:

0–30 minutes  → Fast
31–60 minutes → Medium
60+ minutes   → Slow

How it helps:
This can help a model understand the relationship between delivery speed and customer ratings.

3. Restaurant Order Frequency

Feature: Count how many orders were placed from each restaurant.

Example:

Restaurant A → 50 orders
Restaurant B → 20 orders
Restaurant C → 35 orders

How it helps:
The model can identify frequently used restaurants and learn patterns related to customer preferences and 
restaurant popularity.
