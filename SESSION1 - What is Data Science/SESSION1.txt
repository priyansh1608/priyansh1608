Q - 3 : Imagine you work for a music streaming app like Spotify. Write a short scenario (3-4 sentences)
        describing how data-driven decision making could help the app recommend new songs to users.



Answer : 

Imagine I work for a music streaming app like Spotify. 

The app can analyze a user's listening history, liked songs, skipped songs, artists, and genres to
understand their preferences. 
 
 A machine-learning model can compare this behavior with patterns from 
 other users and identify songs the user may enjoy. 
 
 The app can then recommend new songs and continuously improve the recommendations based on the user's 
 latest interactions.

Q - 4 : Pick one industry from this list: e-commerce, sports, or transportation. Research and describe one
        innovative way data science is being used in that industry today.<br><br><em><strong>Hint:</strong> 
        Search for terms like 'data science in e-commerce' or 'data analytics in sports' to find real examples.
        </em>


Answer : 

Industry: E-Commerce

One innovative use of data science in e-commerce is personalized product recommendation.
E-commerce companies can analyze browsing history, previous purchases, product views, clicks, 
and other interactions to predict which products are most relevant to each customer. Modern 
recommendation systems can also use real-time activity, so recommendations can change as the
customer's interests change.

For example, Amazon Personalize documents recommendation systems that use customer interaction
data to provide personalized recommendations. Its systems can also use contextual information such
as device type or time of day to improve recommendation relevance.

Example: If a customer frequently searches for running shoes and sports clothing, a recommendation 
         system may show running shoes, sports watches, or related accessories. This helps customers 
        discover relevant products more easily while giving the e-commerce company data-driven insights 
        into customer preferences.

Conclusion

Data science is helping e-commerce companies move from showing the same products to everyone toward 
personalized and continuously updated shopping experiences.
